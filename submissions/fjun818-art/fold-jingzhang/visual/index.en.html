Concept Planning & Design Statement

The temporal interweaving from the 19th-century industrial era to the digital-intelligent era of the 2020s-2030s: the design philosophy of "temporal folding" bridging history and the present.

design statement

I. Origins: One Railway, Two Departures

In 1909, the Jingzhang Railway, built under the leadership of Zhan Tianyou, wound its way north from Xizhimen, conquering the Yanshan terrain with its famous "zigzag" alignment and marking a founding coordinate of China's modern industrial civilization — the first time that Chinese engineers independently mastered the organization and construction of modern engineering. In 2019, the Jingzhang High-Speed Railway passed beneath the city with an intelligent 350 km/h service, allowing the same corridor to journey alongside itself across a century — the surface bends of the old railway and the underground straight run of the high-speed line stack vertically within the same urban blocks, forming a "dual-centennial time section" rarely seen in Chinese cities. The Jingzhang corridor addressed by this proposal is precisely where this dual narrative is superimposed upon urban space: to the south lies the fabric of modern industrial heritage — rails, switches, locomotive depots, station buildings and century-old street trees; to the north clusters the contemporary digital-intelligence industry — computing centers, laboratories, data enterprises and scenario testbeds. The spatial legacies and industrial aspirations of two eras spread in parallel along the railway corridor, yet have long remained "adjacent but never intersecting": heritage spaces, lacking contemporary functions, have quietly declined; the emerging industry, lacking cultural anchors and public interfaces, scatters its innovative activities and struggles to congregate. How to bring two parallel timelines into convergence, and to convert heritage value into innovative momentum — this is the fundamental question of the proposal.

II. Philosophy: Temporal Folding, A Dialogue of Two Cities

The proposal puts forward the core concept of the "Jingzhang Digital-Intelligent Folding Belt" — taking "folding" as both a spatial operation and a metaphor of temporal narrative. Just as folding a sheet of paper brings two distant points into contact, the design superimposes the historical scenes of the 19th-century industrial era and the future vision of the 2020s-2030s digital-intelligent era vertically through seven "temporal folding points": the workshop of Qinghuayuan Station merges with the digital-twin base, where computing exhibits and data-art spaces grow beneath the timber trusses of the old station; the switchyard of Gaoliangqiao merges with the data sandbox, turning railway ancillary land into an experimental, simulatable urban data proving ground; the walls of Yuandadu merge with AR narratives, as seven centuries of layered urban fabric develop one by one through the lens of augmented reality. Folding is not demolition and reconstruction but a "light-touch" superimposition: history is not a sealed exhibit but an enterable, interactive and reproducible data element; the future is not groundless imagination but a spatial continuation growing out of the railway fabric. Pedestrians experience "a century in a single step" — by day, the folding points are parks and living rooms for citizens; by night, the AR layer superimposes the whistle and steam of 1909 and the data streams of 2035 within a single viewfinder, sustaining a continuous dialogue between history and the present.

III. Structure: One Belt, One Axis; Two Cores, Multiple Nodes

Spatially, within an overall design scope of approximately 11.4 km², the proposal establishes the structure of "one belt, one axis; two cores, multiple nodes". A cultural narrative belt along the Jingzhang railway heritage green corridor links the seven folding points, organizing dispersed heritage fragments into a continuous narrative; a digital-intelligence industry axis concentrates innovative functions into three key areas: the Zhongzhiyuan/Xuebeiyuan AI Innovation Acceleration Zone in the north, positioned as the "data source", hosting the computing base and R&D incubation on research resources and renewal sites; the Beijing AI Origin Community in the center, positioned as the "folding hub", linking university, industry and residence through community-scaled, life-oriented innovation spaces; the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster in the south, positioned as the "exchange interface", housing the trading lounge and exhibition window around the rail hub. Together the three areas total approximately 368.4 hectares, forming a complete chain of "origin innovation — pilot transformation — circulation and trading". Meanwhile, a blue-green system stitches the urban fabric severed by the railway, and a slow-mobility network connects the four quadrants of Dazhongsi Station, reorganizing under-bridge spaces, linear greenery and pocket parks into a continuous walkable interface — turning "folding" from a concept into everyday experience.

IV. Mechanism: Data Covenant, Rolling Implementation

The proposal operates through the "1+3+N" edge-computing base and the Jingzhang Data Covenant. Historical spaces, once surveyed and digitized, become twin assets; every AR experience and every set of sensor data accumulates into reusable urban data elements. Data ownership, authorization scope and benefit distribution are governed through a three-tier inventory, with enterprises admitted through KYC verification and audit trails, transforming cultural assets into industrial elements along a compliant track. For implementation, eight urban renewal project cards (JZ-01 to JZ-08) cover six fields — public space, new infrastructure, industry services, rail integration, culture-tech and data governance. Each card specifies preconditions (road redlines, ownership, utilities, heritage approvals) and maturity ratings, managed on an "intake — release" basis and rolling forward in step with the five-year transition period to form a "reserve — transformation — implementation" closed loop. JZ-01 slow-mobility stitching, JZ-06 the folding-point experience and JZ-08 the activity route are listed as pilot demonstrations for 2026-2027, validating the mechanism first through small-scale, low-risk moves before extending across the whole area.

V. Vision

We envision the Jingzhang no longer as a railway merely passed by, but as a tangible timeline of the city — where elders read of the former bells of Dazhongsi at the station, children meet the 1909 engineers through AR in the green corridor, entrepreneurs turn data experiments into products at the folding hub, and residents reclaim the public spaces left by the railway in a walkable four-quadrant loop. Here, everyone can encounter the whistle of 1909 and the data streams of 2035 in the same moment; history and future no longer stand still on display, but fulfill each other through folding. This is our answer to the centennial Jingzhang: connecting a century through folding, continuing the Jingzhang narrative through digital intelligence.

(VII) Fold-Node Overview: Time-Space Fold · Seven Anchors

A fold node is an experience interface that presses the century-old railway heritage and the 2035 AI district into one spatial coordinate: along the heritage-park fold belt, AR/MR overlays the 1909 history layer and the 2035 future layer at 7 statutory heritage anchors, forming a computable, perceivable, editable time-space narrative belt.

fold-node overview

Fold-node scenario overview: concept, core functions, application value

Concept

With the heritage park as the fold-belt body (physical layer), the 1+3+N digital-twin base as the editable city (digital layer), and 7 statutory heritage anchors as the experience interface (experience layer), the three-fold structure forms a time-space narrative belt.

Core Functions

Time narration (AI guides revive historical figures), experience overlay (digital-only, no physical change to heritage), data gallery (experimental & urban data visualised), co-creation lounge (developers and public co-create).

Application Value

Cultural inheritance (protection through use), public education (immersive history classroom), industry enablement (AR/MR & cultural-tourism scenarios), public outreach (the Jingzhang 'fold' time-space IP supporting the Global AI Week).
